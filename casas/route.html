What to ask on each visit

Water source and title are the two questions that settle most of the Confirm rows.

  • Water: town network, well, or both? How big is the tank or cistern?
  • Cuts: how often did the water go off in the last two summers, and for how long?
  • Power: does the water depend on a pump? Is there a gravity tank or a generator?
  • Title: is the deed (escritura) ready to sign, or are there only possession rights? On two-lot properties, does each lot have its own deed?
  • Gas: natural gas connected, only at the curb, or bottled?
  • Sewer or septic tank?
  • Streams: how far is the nearest one, and has the lot ever flooded? Matters most in Villa General Belgrano and Villa del Dique.
  • Vegetation: any pines or dense brush on or next to the lot? Matters most at the edges of La Cumbre.
  • New connections: if the house isn't on the town network yet, is the town granting new water connections? La Cumbre suspended them during the 2022–23 drought.
